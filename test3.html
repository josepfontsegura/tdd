Test 3. Mutual information and capacity

Download PDF

The ten questions of the test, each with two options and one correct answer, in the order of the published file; the printed copies had the questions and the options in a different order. Work out your answer before opening a solution.

Question 1. A random symbol X takes values in a finite set of at least two elements. Its entropy H(X) is largest when

(a) the values are equiprobable.
(b) one value has probability one.

Show solution

The correct answer is (a) ★

Section 3.1 gives H(X)\leq\log_2|\mathcal X|, with equality for equiprobable values; one value of probability one gives H(X)=0.

Question 2. On the bit-flipping channel with p=\tfrac12 and equiprobable inputs, the output is an equiprobable bit, so H(Y)=1. The mutual information I(X;Y) is

(a) 1 bit per channel use.
(b) 0.

Show solution

The correct answer is (b) ★

By Proposition 3.4, I(X;Y)=1-h_2(\tfrac12)=0: the uncertainty of the output is all noise, as checked in lecture 5.

Question 3. For a fixed memoryless channel with a finite input alphabet, its capacity is the largest mutual information obtained by varying

(a) the receiver’s decision rule.
(b) the input distribution.

Show solution

The correct answer is (b) ★

Definition 3.7 gives C=\max_{\Pr(x)}I(X;Y): the input probabilities vary, the channel law is fixed, as in Seminar 5.

Question 4. For a memoryless channel of capacity C and a fixed rate R<C, the coding theorem says that

(a) there are codes of rate R, long enough, with error probability as small as we wish.
(b) every code of rate R has error probability as small as we wish if it is long enough.

Show solution

The correct answer is (a) ★

Theorem 3.9 is an existence result: good codes exist at every rate below capacity, at the price of their length.

Question 5. Let Y=X+Z, where Z has a density and is independent of X. Assume the differential entropies below are finite. Changing the input distribution

(a) can change both h(Y) and h(Y|X).
(b) can change h(Y), but leaves h(Y|X)=h(Z) unchanged.

Show solution

The correct answer is (b) ★

Section 3.4 gives h(Y|X)=h(Z) for every input law, so by (3.24) only h(Y) depends on the input, as in Seminar 5.

Question 6. For discrete random variables X and Y, the mutual information I(X;Y) measures

(a) the average reduction in uncertainty about X when Y is observed.
(b) the average uncertainty about X that remains after Y is observed.

Show solution

The correct answer is (a) ★

Section 3.2 gives I(X;Y)=H(X)-H(X|Y), the uncertainty removed by observing Y. The remaining uncertainty is H(X|Y).

Question 7. The differential entropy of a real random variable with a density

(a) is always nonnegative.
(b) can be negative.

Show solution

The correct answer is (b) ★

Example 3.11 gives h(U)=\log_2 a for a uniform variable on an interval of length a, negative when a<1.

Question 8. A real Gaussian channel has noise of variance \sigma^2 and input constraint \EE(X^2)\leq\Es. With \snr=\Es/\sigma^2, its capacity in bits per channel use is

(a) \tfrac12\log_2(1+\snr).
(b) \log_2(1+\snr).

Show solution

The correct answer is (a) ★

Section 3.5 gives \tfrac12\log_2(1+\snr) for the real channel; the other option is the formula of the complex channel.

Question 9. A channel input takes M values. Whatever the channel and the input probabilities, I(X;Y)

(a) is at most \log_2M, since I(X;Y)\leq H(X).
(b) can exceed \log_2M if the noise is small enough.

Show solution

The correct answer is (a) ★

Sections 3.2 and 3.1 give I(X;Y)\leq H(X)\leq\log_2M; in Seminar 5, two inputs reach this ceiling of 1 bit once d\geq1.

Question 10. Among real random variables with a density and a given positive variance, the largest differential entropy is attained by

(a) a uniform distribution.
(b) a Gaussian distribution.

Show solution

The correct answer is (b) ★

Theorem 3.13 states that the Gaussian maximizes differential entropy at a given variance. The uniform distribution maximizes it under a fixed bounded-support constraint instead.
