Modality A

With midterm mark M and final examination mark E, both on a scale of 0–10, the course mark F is

F=0.625(0.20M+0.80E).

The midterm has twelve true/false statements and lasts 30 minutes. Statements draw on parts of the published theory collection and may be paraphrased. The final lasts two hours, with four options per problem and a required mathematical justification.

Modality B

Let E be the final examination mark, S the seminar average, X the transversal assignment mark and T the test average. Seminars and the assignment form the submissions component D=(5S+X)/6, which weighs 30%. If E\geq5, the course mark is

F=0.60E+0.30D+0.10T=0.60E+0.25S+0.05X+0.10T.

If E<5, then F=E. The transversal assignment contributes 5%; its task and submission arrangements will be announced separately.

Seminars consist of seven questions with three options and a written justification, in groups of two or three. Five individual tests cover Topics 1–5; Topic 6 has no class test. Each test lasts 15 minutes and has ten questions with two options each.

Classroom activities

Seminar answers and explanations are written in English. Printed lecture notes may be used in seminars; tests are taken without notes. Electronic devices, calculators, AI tools and online help are not allowed during these activities. No electronic device may be in sight. If a device is detected, the student receives a grade of zero in the test, and in a seminar all the members of the group receive a grade of zero.

The online exercises and quizzes are for independent practice and do not contribute to your course mark.
