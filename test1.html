Test 1. Detection

Download PDF

The ten questions of the test, each with two options and one correct answer, in the order of the published file; the printed copies had the questions and the options in a different order. Work out your answer before opening a solution.

Notation: \Pr(x) is the input distribution and W(y|x) the channel law.

Question 1. On a discrete channel, the joint probability of sending x and receiving y is

(a) \Pr(x)\,\Pr(y).
(b) \Pr(x)\,W(y|x).

Show solution

The correct answer is (b) ★

The product rule of Table 1.1 gives \Pr(x,y)=\Pr(x)W(y|x). Multiplying the two marginals would require independence of the input and output.

Question 2. For arbitrary input probabilities, the MAP detector minimizes the average error probability by choosing the symbol that maximizes

(a) \Pr(x)\,W(y|x).
(b) W(y|x).

Show solution

The correct answer is (a) ★

Definition 1.3 and Theorem 1.4 give the MAP rule and its optimality. For a fixed observation, the joint probability is proportional to the posterior probability \Pr(x|y).

Question 3. For a fixed observation y, the ML detector compares

(a) the posterior probabilities \Pr(x|y).
(b) the likelihoods W(y|x).

Show solution

The correct answer is (b) ★

By Definition 1.3, ML maximizes W(y|x) and does not use the input probabilities. MAP maximizes the posterior probability.

Question 4. With equiprobable symbols on a Gaussian channel, both MAP and ML choose the constellation point that is

(a) nearest to the observation.
(b) nearest to the origin.

Show solution

The correct answer is (a) ★

Equal priors make MAP and ML coincide. By Proposition 1.5, the Gaussian likelihood decreases with the distance from the observation to the candidate symbol.

Question 5. With the channel and detector fixed, changing the input probabilities leaves the conditional error probability P_{\rm e}(x)

(a) unchanged.
(b) dependent on the new prior probability of x.

Show solution

The correct answer is (a) ★

By Definition 1.2 and the following remark, P_{\rm e}(x) conditions on x being sent. It depends on the channel and fixed detector, not on how often x is sent.

Question 6. Two symbols x_0 and x_1 have probabilities q and 1-q. The average error probability of a detector is

(a) P_{\rm e}(x_0)+P_{\rm e}(x_1).
(b) qP_{\rm e}(x_0)+(1-q)P_{\rm e}(x_1).

Show solution

The correct answer is (b) ★

Equation (1.7) weights each conditional error probability by the probability of sending that symbol.

Question 7. A symbol X is chosen equiprobably from \{-1,1,-j,j\}. Its average symbol energy \Es is

(a) 1.
(b) 0.

Show solution

The correct answer is (a) ★

Section 1.2 defines \Es=\EE(|X|^2). Every symbol has squared magnitude 1, so \Es=1. The mean symbol \EE(X) is zero, but the average energy is not.

Question 8. Equiprobable symbols \pm A, with A>0, are sent over the real Gaussian channel. In terms of the SNR, the ML error probability is

(a) \tfrac12\erfc\bigl(\sqrt{2\snr}\bigr).
(b) \tfrac12\erfc\bigl(\sqrt{\snr/2}\bigr).

Show solution

The correct answer is (b) ★

For 2-PAM, \Es=A^2 and \snr=A^2/\sigma^2. Equation (1.22) gives P_{\rm e}=\tfrac12\erfc(\sqrt{\snr/2}).

Question 9. For a fixed 2-PAM constellation on the real Gaussian channel, increasing the SNR makes the ML error probability

(a) increase.
(b) decrease.

Show solution

The correct answer is (b) ★

Equation (1.22) gives P_{\rm e}=\tfrac12\erfc(\sqrt{\snr/2}). The function \erfc decreases with its argument, so the error probability falls as the SNR increases.

Question 10. For equiprobable QPSK symbols \pm a\pm ja, with a>0, on the complex Gaussian channel, an ML error when x=a+ja is sent occurs if the output has

(a) a negative real or imaginary part.
(b) a negative real part and a negative imaginary part.

Show solution

The correct answer is (a) ★

In Figure 1.2(b) and Problem 1.13, the region of a+ja is the first quadrant. At least one wrong sign causes an error. The axes have probability zero.
